# Non-Official Benchmarks

Industry conversion benchmarks are not Apple specifications and are not universal targets. If a task uses one, record the publisher and direct URL, dataset/storefront scope, metric definition and denominator, publication and verification dates, segment relevance, and known bias.

Do not ship a benchmark number merely because it appears in an older ASO guide. Prefer the app's own App Store Connect baseline and controlled product page experiments. Apple-published observations may be cited as Apple-published data, but they still do not guarantee an individual app's result.
